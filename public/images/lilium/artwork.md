# Lilium specimen assets

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. These are original photographic
cutouts for the scroll study; the supplied screenshots guided the composition
and animation, and are not shipped as frames.

- `specimen.png`: full red lily and stem, with transparent alpha.
- `bud.png`: unopened lily bud, with transparent alpha.

## Specimen prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: transparent botanical specimen cutout for an interactive website. Generate ONE single scarlet-red lily in full bloom, facing the camera, six naturally curled red petals, prominent central green pistil with burgundy stigma and six slender stamens with dark reddish anthers. Long slender green stem gently curved in an S below the blossom, no leaves, no buds, no vase. Realistic delicate longitudinal petal veins, rich crimson shadows and coral-red highlights. Scientific studio specimen photography, crisp focus, soft diffused lighting, beautifully natural slightly asymmetric flower. Composition: portrait 2:3, whole flower and stem visible with generous transparent margin, blossom occupies top 50% of image width 80%, stem extends down to 92% of image. Important: genuine transparent alpha background, no white or black backdrop, no checkerboard, no shadow, no text, no borders, no annotations, no watermark. Final flower will be isolated over both ivory and black, close-cropped to the blossom and recolored to cyan negatives with code. Supply a high-resolution clean photographic cutout.

## Bud prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: website botanical cutout. One single unopened red lily bud, elongated tapered bud pointing diagonally up and right, olive-green sepals with deep crimson streaks and a short green stalk. Extreme botanical studio macro, real fine veined texture, crisp detail, soft directional lighting. Square canvas with the whole bud centered, occupying 80 percent of frame. Genuine transparent alpha background. No text, no labels, no watermark, no frame, no other objects. This isolated bud will appear in a black anatomy image card.
